I'm Diego Lemos de Resende, author of this blog.

I began my studies in Computer Science at [Universidade Federal de Uberlândia](http://www.ufu.br/), Brazil, back in 2004. Later on, I moved to France where I obtained a master's degree at [Institut National des Sciences Appliquées de Lyon](https://www.insa-lyon.fr/). Currently, I work and live in London, UK.

During my career, I've worked for start-ups, software houses, consultancy companies and investment banks in Brazil, France and the United Kingdom as software engineer, engineering lead, product manager, etc.

As a hobby, I like snowboarding and I try to spend at least a week on snow every year. I also like to travel and discover new places and cultures. Last but not least, I am a big fan of good coffee and wine.

Every opinion in this blog is strictly personal.
